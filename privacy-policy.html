PulseStamp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

PulseStamp is made by Akshay Kumar Saha, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What PulseStamp reads

With your permission, PulseStamp reads:

  • From Samsung Health (through the Samsung Health Data SDK): your workouts (start time, end time, type) and the GPS route of a workout.
  • From Health Connect: your workouts, workout routes, heart rate and speed.

It reads this data only to build the GPX file you ask for. It never writes to Samsung Health or Health Connect.

What PulseStamp does with it

The app combines the route and heart rate of the workout you pick into a GPX file and saves it to your phone's Downloads folder. You can then import that file into the Insta360 app or any other app you choose.

What PulseStamp does not do

  • It has no internet access. The INTERNET permission is removed from the app, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • It has no account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not keep a copy of your health data. Android backup is turned off for the app.

A file leaves your phone only if you share it yourself.

Your choices

You can withdraw permissions at any time in Samsung Health (Settings › Data permissions) or in Health Connect. You can delete exported GPX files from your Downloads folder. Uninstalling the app removes everything it holds.

Children

PulseStamp is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
